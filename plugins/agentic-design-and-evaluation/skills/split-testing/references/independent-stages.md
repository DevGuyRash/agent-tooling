# Independent Stages

An agent-run comparison separates defining success, designing its evidence, performing the work, judging outcomes, and presenting accepted findings. The orchestrator SHALL arrange these responsibilities in the order below, with correction returning to the decision whose grounds changed. This is an execution arrangement for independent contributions and review; it does not prescribe a participant's private reasoning process.

Define good → approve the definition → construct the rubric and evidence design → approve them → execute → assess and adjudicate → visualize and verify.

The orchestrator SHALL choose the number of alternatives, cases, rounds, workers, ordinary reviewers, and adversarial reviewers for the actual uncertainty and resources. Distinct responsibilities require distinct contributions where independence is specified; there is no fixed panel size, round count, scoring scale, or quota of objections. Fresh contexts can run sequentially when concurrency is limited. A focused assignment can use the applicable portion of this arrangement and retained approved inputs without reenacting completed work. Missing required independence or evidence SHALL remain an explicit limitation rather than being relabeled as satisfied.

Approval here is an evidential decision within delegated authority, not a requirement to ask the user at every stage. The orchestrator SHALL seek a decision from the rightful owner when the unresolved matter exceeds that authority. Participants SHALL receive the original assignment, relevant constraints, authoritative inputs, and legitimate research access needed for their role. A summary can orient access but SHALL NOT replace consequential original grounds. [Information and execution](information-and-execution.md) owns the input, freshness, separation, and retention boundaries.

## Define Good

The orchestrator SHALL commission independent proposals of what adequate and preferable outcomes mean for the intended use. Proposers SHALL receive the original assignment and constraints, relevant authoritative sources, and research access without sibling proposals or a controller's preferred definition. They SHALL preserve a concise initial position based on their available knowledge before researching the applicable standards and practice. The initial position is a reviewable proposal with its assumptions and uncertainties, not an account of private thought.

Each proposer SHALL then investigate relevant sources and retain a supported revised proposal, including the grounds for consequential changes or retained positions. Research SHALL distinguish requirements that actually govern the assignment, established standards and their scope, conventional practice, and contested recommendations. Source identity, applicability, and material disagreement SHALL remain recoverable. External practice can support a proposal or expose a conflict; it SHALL NOT silently supersede the original assignment or acquire authority merely by being called a standard. Unavailable or inconclusive research SHALL leave the affected proposal qualified.

Proposals SHOULD connect adequacy and preference to the consumer's purpose, legitimate alternative outcomes, meaningful tradeoffs, and circumstances in which reliance would fail. They SHALL leave genuinely open choices open and preserve informed requirements. Agreement with an existing definition is a valid supported proposal.

## Review, Adjudicate, and Finalize Approvals

The orchestrator SHALL use this arrangement for substantive approval of the definition, rubric and evidence design, and assessed findings, including consequential revisions. Ordinary review and adversarial review SHALL begin independently: reviewers receive the original grounds and complete relevant proposals and research, but no sibling reviews or preferred verdict. The adversarial contribution SHALL actively test important assumptions, omissions, criteria, and inferences against credible counterevidence and legitimate variations. It SHALL be free to conclude that the work is sound. Neither opposition nor consensus is a success target.

Reviewers SHALL retain their initial contributions before discussion or corrective feedback. Their recommendations MAY support inclusion, revision, exclusion, missing material, or an unresolved dependency; the form remains appropriate to their evidence. Follow-up discussion SHALL remain distinguishable from those initial contributions. Each agent review round, including follow-up rounds, SHALL retain an independent adversarial contribution. The orchestrator SHOULD expand review when missing expertise, coverage, or an unresolved interpretation can materially affect the decision.

For each substantive approval stage, the orchestrator SHALL assign a fresh adjudicator who did not produce or review the material being decided. The adjudicator SHALL receive the original assignment and constraints, authoritative sources, complete relevant initial and revised proposals, research, recommendations, objections, and native evidence. A controller's summary or vote tally SHALL NOT be the sole account. The adjudicator SHALL reconstruct the decision from these grounds and record which positions are accepted, rejected, conditional, or unresolved, with concise reasons and source connections. These distinctions need not be a table or a prescribed report schema.

Recommendations and votes inform adjudication; original requirements, authority, and evidence justify it. The adjudicator SHALL NOT resolve a factual dispute by majority count, reviewer confidence, or the adversarial label. Material disputes SHALL receive targeted investigation capable of changing the decision or remain explicitly unresolved with their effect on reliance. A new material finding SHALL return to independent ordinary and adversarial review before approval; unaffected findings need not be reconsidered. An unresolved value or permission decision belongs with its legitimate owner.

After adjudication, the orchestrator SHALL assign a separate fresh finalizer to organize, clarify, and deduplicate the accepted material. The finalizer SHALL receive the original grounds, complete relevant contributions, and adjudication decisions so that meaning and traceability survive compilation. It SHALL preserve accepted distinctions, conditions, and unresolved dependencies. It SHALL NOT add criteria, discard an accepted distinction, change judgments, or silently decide a conflict. A substantive omission or contradiction SHALL return to adjudication; presentation changes can remain with the finalizer. The orchestrator SHALL check the compiled version against the decisions before dependent work uses it. If an agent review round performs that check, ordinary and independent adversarial contributions remain required.

Approval SHALL identify the version and scope used by dependent work, along with conditions and unresolved limitations. It freezes a usable reference, not immunity to correction. The orchestrator SHALL preserve the prior version and its grounds when a material correction is warranted, identify affected dependencies, and rerun only the decisions or observations whose support changed. Approval cannot authorize effects outside the original mandate.

## Construct and Approve the Rubric and Evidence Design

After definition approval, the orchestrator SHALL assign fresh rubric and evidence designers the approved definition, original requirements, authoritative inputs, accumulated relevant research, and legitimate access needed to design the comparison. They SHALL NOT receive candidate execution results or preferred rankings while developing the prospective design. They SHALL NOT receive sibling proposals before their own independent contributions are retained. Available knowledge of alternatives can inform the comparison's scope; prior outcome exposure SHALL remain identifiable and cannot count as independent prospective design.

Designers SHALL use the accumulated research and SHOULD investigate further when a material uncertainty needs it; adequate supplied sources do not require a new research cycle. They SHALL retain their initial independent proposals and any subsequent revisions. They SHALL derive criteria, informative cases, observations, and assessment methods that can distinguish adequacy, legitimate variation, consequential failure, and preference under the approved definition. The design SHALL make consequential inclusion, allocation, assistance, retry, selection, and stopping decisions usable before the outcomes they govern are inspected. [Arranging evidence](arranging-evidence.md) supplies the comparative design guidance.

The rubric MAY be qualitative, quantitative, or mixed. Designers SHALL NOT impose a universal numerical scale or collapse unresolved values into invented weights. They SHALL qualify consequential checks against both plausible inadequate outcomes and legitimately different adequate outcomes, deriving the intended verdict from original requirements and evidence. A check that merely rewards its own favored representation is not qualified by repeating its assumptions. Existing evidence or an adequate domain argument can supply this qualification; limits SHALL remain explicit.

The orchestrator SHALL send these proposals and their research through independent ordinary and adversarial review, fresh adjudication, and separate fresh finalization before candidate execution relies on the design. Defects in the approved definition SHALL return to its approval stage rather than being silently repaired inside a rubric. Diagnostic work informed by later candidate outcomes SHALL be identified as such, with prospective confirmation where the claim needs it.

## Execute, Assess, and Adjudicate

Workers SHALL receive the original assignment, their candidate materials, genuine constraints, and legitimate support for the intended use. They SHALL NOT receive private grading answers, expected rankings, sibling outcomes, or evaluator-only instructions that perform work the candidate is meant to do. The orchestrator SHALL preserve their native outcomes, exposed execution records, relevant condition facts, and any assistance or collection failure under [information and execution](information-and-execution.md).

Fresh assessors SHALL receive approved criteria and evidence design, original requirements and authoritative sources, and the native outcomes to be assessed. Their initial assessments SHALL be independent of sibling assessments and preferred conclusions. They SHALL judge from the approved grounds while retaining a route to challenge a defective criterion or missing evidence. Self-assessments and author opinions can be evidence for review but SHALL NOT substitute for the required fresh assessment.

Separate ordinary and adversarial review SHALL challenge consequential assessments before fresh adjudication. The adjudicator SHALL apply the approval arrangement above, using [validity and attribution](validity-and-attribution.md) to resolve factual premises, uncertain measurements, and inference limits. A faulty check returns to rubric approval; an inadequate observation returns to evidence design or execution; a changed definition returns to definition approval. The responsible stage SHALL correct the affected downstream conclusions while retaining earlier observations and the grounds for revision.

The orchestrator SHOULD add alternatives, cases, executions, or independent judgments only for a consequential gap that the added work can address. Additional participants cannot supply an unavailable observation merely by agreeing. Continuation after feedback is refinement; an independent confirmation needs a fresh, appropriately unexposed contribution and evidence suitable for the claim. A supported tie, conditional choice, rejection of the supplied options, or unresolved comparison can be the correct adjudicated finding.

## Visualize and Verify

A fresh visual finalizer SHALL receive the approved definition and rubric, adjudicated findings, relevant native evidence, and limitations. It SHALL choose and assemble a useful presentation under [presenting evidence](presenting-evidence.md), preserving analytical decisions and their evidence. It SHALL NOT invent scores, rankings, weights, or uncertainty estimates, or revise judgments through presentation choices.

The orchestrator SHALL arrange independent ordinary and adversarial review of visual fidelity. Review SHALL inspect whether layout, aggregation, color, ordering, omitted cases, labels, or interaction changes the apparent conclusion or conceals a consequential limit. Presentation defects return to the visual finalizer; substantive defects return to the responsible adjudication stage. Further review rounds retain the same independence requirement. The final deliverable SHALL preserve the approved meaning, expose the evidence needed for its use, and pass the interface checks its delivery claims require.
