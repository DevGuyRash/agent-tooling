# Validity and Attribution

You SHOULD resolve a property through the nearest legitimate evidence and authority, such as original sources, executable state, downstream outcomes, qualified expertise, actual users, or the rightful decision owner's values. You SHOULD use an acceptance relation when several outcomes satisfy the task rather than forcing one reference answer.

You SHOULD establish why each consequential criterion and observation bears on the actual requirement. A necessary condition or correlated signal may help without being sufficient. Keep hard requirements distinguishable from preferences, and judge qualitative work against its intended audience and purpose without requiring a preferred wording or arbitrary universal score. If a proxy is the best available evidence, preserve the uncertainty in the connection to the claimed outcome.

Every checker, simulation, rubric, reviewer, and interface is partial. Confidence, agreement, automation, consistency, or secrecy does not give an instrument authority. Qualification includes the original premises, reference products, extraction, normalization, aggregation, and other dependencies that determine what the check can observe. A comparison of transformed values cannot recover a distinction the transformation erased. Shared unsupported premises can make both generator and evaluator agree incorrectly.

Where model judgment materially determines the conclusion, you SHALL obtain the fresh assessment and separate ordinary and adversarial review required by [independent stages](independent-stages.md), grounded in the original task, approved criteria, and native outcomes. Select reviewer capabilities and access for the uncertainty they can resolve, and qualify consequential reviewer judgments as well as executable checks. Different names, sessions, or models can share the same blind spot. The candidate's self-assessment and its author's preferred rubric can inform review without defining correctness.

Review needs the legitimate claim, native evidence, applicable authority, admissible sources, and the consumer consequence. You SHALL leave a route for decisive evidence a rubric did not anticipate. Ordinary and adversarial reviewers SHALL prepare their initial contributions independently and retain them before discussion; sound work is a valid finding in either role. When reviewers disagree materially, the fresh adjudicator SHALL identify the contested requirement, observation, inference, or preference and seek evidence capable of resolving it or preserve the uncertainty and its consequence. Further reviewers are useful when they add relevant expertise, coverage, or an independent error-detection opportunity. A vote measures responses, not truth or permission; neither majority agreement nor one confident objection settles a factual dispute. Preserve unresolved value differences as conditional choices. You SHALL verify decisive factual premises against retained evidence capable of correcting them before using the judgment in a conclusion.

When a consequential premise or measurement fails, you SHALL reconsider the tests, judgments, comparisons, and recommendations that depended on it. Reassess affected native outcomes under the corrected interpretation where possible, treating comparable conditions consistently. Preserve judgments whose grounds remain sound and identify missing observations that prevent correction. A repaired candidate is a new condition; its success does not repair an invalid grader or retroactively validate the earlier result. Verify the affected relationships, including legitimate behavior the repair could disturb, before relying on the correction.

You SHALL keep mechanical termination, infrastructure behavior, task validity, candidate behavior, instrument validity, and causal explanation distinct where collapsing them changes the decision. An incoherent task does not establish candidate failure. A valid task failure under the tested policy remains evidence even if a different policy could rescue it. Capture failure is not success or automatic permission to rerun; you SHALL preserve missing and replaced observations under the declared policy.

You SHALL distinguish outcomes from mechanisms. You SHALL assign a cause only when evidence separates it from plausible alternatives and that attribution matters. Intervention, ablation, counterfactual contrast, or fresh confirmation can support stronger repair or transfer claims. A changed prompt followed by success alone does not isolate the change's contribution.

You SHALL preserve differences in populations, time horizons, operating conditions, constraints, tails, interactions, and uncertainty when collapsing them could change action. Assess practical benefit alongside variability, failures, and effort relevant to use. A favorable average or highest observed score can conceal an unacceptable condition or a difference too small to matter. Statistical significance does not establish usefulness, and failure to detect a difference does not establish equivalence. Aggregates can help but cannot erase a veto or supply absent preference authority. You SHALL report non-identifiability when the available evidence cannot distinguish causes or rank the live alternatives. Narrowing a claim cannot erase verification that the task actually requires.
