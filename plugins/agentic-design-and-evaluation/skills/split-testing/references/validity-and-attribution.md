# Validity and Attribution

You SHOULD resolve a property through the nearest legitimate evidence and authority, such as original sources, executable state, downstream outcomes, qualified expertise, actual users, or the rightful decision owner's values. You SHOULD use an acceptance relation when several outcomes satisfy the task rather than forcing one reference answer.

Every checker, simulation, rubric, reviewer, and interface is partial. Confidence, agreement, automation, consistency, or secrecy does not give an instrument authority. Before relying on a consequential instrument, you SHOULD examine plausible defects that could reverse the choice, hide a non-compensable failure, or make the property unobservable. Shared unsupported premises can make both generator and evaluator agree incorrectly.

Review needs the legitimate claim, native evidence, applicable authority, admissible sources, and the consumer consequence. You SHOULD leave a route for decisive evidence a rubric did not anticipate. Reviewer count and separation follow the actual uncertainty; a vote measures responses, not truth or permission. You SHALL verify decisive factual premises against retained evidence capable of correcting them before using the judgment in a conclusion.

You SHALL keep mechanical termination, infrastructure behavior, task validity, candidate behavior, instrument validity, and causal explanation distinct where collapsing them changes the decision. An incoherent task does not establish candidate failure. A valid task failure under the tested policy remains evidence even if a different policy could rescue it. Capture failure is not success or automatic permission to rerun; you SHALL preserve missing and replaced observations under the declared policy.

You SHALL distinguish outcomes from mechanisms. You SHALL assign a cause only when evidence separates it from plausible alternatives and that attribution matters. Intervention, ablation, counterfactual contrast, or fresh confirmation can support stronger repair or transfer claims. A changed prompt followed by success alone does not isolate the change's contribution.

You SHALL preserve differences in populations, time horizons, operating conditions, constraints, tails, interactions, and uncertainty when collapsing them could change action. Aggregates can help but cannot erase a veto or supply absent preference authority. You SHALL report non-identifiability when the available evidence cannot distinguish causes or rank the live alternatives. Narrowing a claim cannot erase verification that the task actually requires.
