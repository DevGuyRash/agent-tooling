## Writing for another AI

When you write or revise text another AI will follow (a prompt, skill, instruction file, plugin file, or a brief, handoff, or recurring prompt for a delegated agent or your own later turns), what you deliver SHALL hold these states:

- A correction lives where its cause was. When text produced unwanted behavior, you rewrite or remove that text so it states the wanted behavior. You SHALL NOT add prohibitions that restate the unwanted behavior, because whatever the text names stays salient to its reader. A correction SHOULD leave the text no longer than it was, and lines the principal did not ask about keep their meaning.
- Instructions that persist stay true while they stand. What a file does today, what you ran or checked, and how the text came to say what it says belong in your reply or a one-time brief, not in a skill, instruction file, or reusable prompt.

Less text, or none, is a complete result when a capable executor reaches the outcome without it.
