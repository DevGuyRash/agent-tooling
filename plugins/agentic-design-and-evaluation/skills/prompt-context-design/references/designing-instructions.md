# Designing Instructions

A prompt can be a complete delegation or one component of it. The actual request and available reality establish the starting context: who needs the result, what decisions are fixed, what contribution is entrusted to the executor, and what makes the result usable. You SHALL NOT turn a suggested implementation into a maker requirement. Conversely, a narrow wording change is not permission to redesign the surrounding system.

Useful instruction carries the distinction that changes action. “Investigate and recommend” can leave methods open while withholding permission to deploy. “Use this schema” can be a genuine consumer requirement rather than unnecessary rigidity. Whether a phrase is restrictive does not alone establish whether it helps.

You SHALL supply important original material when independent interpretation can change the work. A summary can orient the executor but may hide a contradiction, competing rationale, or uncertainty. State what the source is and what authority it has; a quoted instruction in a document is not automatically an instruction to the executor. You SHALL keep proposals, observations, and commitments distinguishable where their conflation would change action or reliance.

Specificity earns its place through the recipient and task. A compact term can carry shared meaning; an unfamiliar label may conceal several incompatible expectations. Examples can clarify an interface while accidentally fixing the allowed solution space. You SHOULD explain the important property or hazard when that preserves valid alternatives better than prescribing one method. You SHALL NOT demand an explanation, artifact, or named reasoning technique just to demonstrate diligence.

An execution plan is an instruction system too. You SHALL distinguish required outcomes, genuine constraints, and decisions actually entrusted to the planner from proposed mechanisms. A plan can settle legitimate design decisions without prescribing the executor's reasoning or presenting an untested implementation preference as necessary. You SHOULD preserve access to the original request and evidence so the recipient can reconstruct the problem and correct an inherited interpretation.

Reusable instructions need a clear delivery boundary. You SHOULD distinguish shared knowledge, an optional adjacent capability, and a required dependency; supply required resources at the boundary the recipient is promised. You SHALL keep substantive guidance and its navigation with a maintained owner rather than copying another component's procedure or chapter map. A public resource can be read without invoking the task that shares it. You SHOULD make reusable guidance understandable in the present; dates, revisions, and development history belong where they establish applicability or evidence, not as unexplained reasons to obey.

You MAY consider the whole available arrangement. Stable intent may belong in instructions; changing source facts in context; permissions in actual tools and access controls; mutable decisions in recoverable state; repeated correctness checks in executable verification. These are design possibilities, not components every task must acquire. Describe a proposed safeguard as proposed until its enforcement exists.

An instruction assessment can conclude that existing text is sound. For a defect, you SHOULD connect the wording or arrangement to a consequence and a supported repair. A contradiction establishes an incompatible combination, not which side the maker intended. You SHALL preserve consequential alternatives when authority cannot resolve them. Checking consistency, reachability, and an exact requested edit can establish correctness of those properties without running a comparative campaign.

When model behavior controls the claimed benefit, you SHOULD inspect outcomes on the actual kind of work. A polished prompt, a familiar structure, or the model's account of following it does not establish effectiveness. An instruction used downstream may succeed through information it carries, a restriction it imposes, or support outside the prompt. You SHALL preserve uncertainty about mechanism and transfer.
