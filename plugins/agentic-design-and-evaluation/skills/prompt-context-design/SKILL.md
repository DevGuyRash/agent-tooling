# Prompt and Context Design

You SHALL produce the instruction, context arrangement, assessment, or other contribution the user needs. A narrow edit can be the whole assignment. When selecting the intervention is delegated, you MAY consider whether changed information, tools, permissions, continuity, or no new artifact would serve the purpose. You SHALL preserve informed choices and make ordinary design decisions within the mandate.

When creating or revising instructions for another AI, you SHALL apply the shared [governing architecture](../foundational-knowledge/references/governing-architecture.md). It governs authoring decisions, not a required sequence or output template. Assessment of an external instruction does not make that target subject to this charter.

The complete [foundational knowledge](../foundational-knowledge/references/foundational-knowledge.md) informs judgment. Its contents and Key Takeaways provide orientation: sections 3–6 concern delegation, authority, and communication; 7–8 concern representation and grounding; 9–11 concern continuity and correction; 13–17 concern evaluation and transfer. You SHOULD read the discussion relevant to the task, including its qualifications, and broaden when another concern becomes material. These are nonexclusive entry points, not prerequisites to every small edit. You MAY reuse unchanged material still available in context; you SHOULD recover relevant grounds after context loss or source change.

For substantial design, [designing instructions](references/designing-instructions.md) discusses intent, authority, and useful specificity. For source selection, retrieval, attachments, or context pressure, you MAY use [arranging context](references/arranging-context.md). For ongoing work and delegation, you MAY use [continuation and handoffs](references/continuation-and-handoffs.md). You SHOULD read only what contributes to the assignment.

Instruction content for a skill belongs here too. If packaging matters, you MAY consult the sibling resources on [portable skills](../skill-auditor/references/open-standard.md) and [host delivery](../skill-auditor/references/host-contracts.md) directly; compatible scaffolding can help without starting an audit. A genuine comparative investigation uses the canonical [Split Testing](../split-testing/SKILL.md) guidance. Ordinary draft checking does not require another workflow.

You SHALL deliver usable work at its own boundary. A downstream executor needs the relevant meaning, authority, source material or reliable access, and completion interface without depending on this authoring conversation or this plugin. You SHOULD include knowledge when it helps that executor; you SHOULD NOT copy the full foundation by default. You SHOULD check the consequential properties of what is actually delivered and distinguish those checks from a demonstrated improvement in model behavior.
