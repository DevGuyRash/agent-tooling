# Prompt and Context Design

You SHALL produce the instruction, context arrangement, assessment, or other contribution the user needs. A narrow edit can be the whole assignment. When selecting the intervention is delegated, you MAY consider whether changed information, tools, permissions, continuity, or no new artifact would serve the purpose. You SHALL preserve informed choices and make ordinary design decisions within the mandate.

When creating or revising instructions for another AI, you SHALL apply the shared [governing architecture](../foundational-knowledge/references/governing-architecture.md). It governs authoring decisions, not a required sequence or output template. Assessment of an external instruction does not make that target subject to this charter.

The complete [foundational knowledge](../foundational-knowledge/references/foundational-knowledge.md) supplies the grounds for delegation, context, and independent understanding. Its own contents guide access to the relevant discussion. This and the charter are public resources supplied by the complete plugin; if a required resource is unavailable, you SHALL expose the incomplete installation and its effect on the assignment rather than claim that its guidance was applied.

For substantial instruction or execution-plan design, [designing instructions](references/designing-instructions.md) addresses intent, authority, and useful specificity. For source selection, retrieval, attachments, or context pressure, use [arranging context](references/arranging-context.md). For ongoing work and delegation, use [continuation and handoffs](references/continuation-and-handoffs.md). You SHOULD read only what contributes to the assignment.

Instruction content for a skill belongs here too. The public references on [portable skills](../skill-auditor/references/open-standard.md) and [host delivery](../skill-auditor/references/host-contracts.md) supply packaging knowledge without starting an audit. A genuine comparative investigation uses [Split Testing](../split-testing/SKILL.md). Ordinary draft checking remains part of authoring.

You SHALL deliver usable work at its own boundary. A downstream executor needs the relevant meaning, authority, source material or reliable access, and completion interface without depending on this authoring conversation or this plugin. You SHOULD include knowledge when it helps that executor; you SHOULD NOT copy the full foundation by default. You SHOULD check the consequential properties of what is actually delivered and distinguish those checks from a demonstrated improvement in model behavior.
