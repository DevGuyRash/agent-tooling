# Self-Healing

You SHALL serve the assignment through a supported improvement or a grounded decision to preserve the current arrangement. Establish the intended result, relevant original sources, actual consumer and authority.

Preserve consequential observations and native evidence while their context is available. You SHOULD retain an initial account before elaborating a cause or consulting related interpretations, when feasible within the task. Keep the status of recalled expectations clear. Use free-form Markdown and the available file tools where persistent records help; retain original accounts and link subsequent findings to them.

You SHALL ground corrective changes in repeatable evidence appropriate to the claimed problem. Choose relevant conditions, observations and stopping grounds before inspecting their results, and preserve justified amendments. Examine the requirement and the instrument alongside the apparent failure. [Investigation and correction](references/investigation.md) provides additional grounds when these distinctions affect the work.

Choose the intervention that serves the consumer. Assess whether a tool, instruction, repeated operation or other support earns its setup, context and maintenance costs. Keep context-dependent interpretation and decisions with the executor; use mechanisms for operations whose defined contract improves reliability or reduces repeated effort. Simplification, removal, better information and direct execution remain available contributions.

Consult [Split Testing](../split-testing/SKILL.md) when a comparative decision needs its methods. Use [foundational knowledge](../foundational-knowledge/references/foundational-knowledge.md) where its distinctions help, and apply the [governing architecture](../foundational-knowledge/references/governing-architecture.md) when authoring instructions for another AI.

You SHALL act within the assignment's authority, repair the maintained source, preserve unrelated work and check current inputs before applying a prepared change. Separate maintenance receives its own purpose, targets, evidence access and limits.

Use independent reproduction or challenge when it materially improves reliance and participation is available. An organized agent review round SHALL include ordinary and independent adversarial contributions. Preserve initial contributions before feedback and retain delegated assignments, findings and native artifacts.

You SHALL verify the affected behavior and material dependencies at the actual consumer. Continue while the assignment requires useful work, and finish when its result and required checks are supported. Preserve concrete gaps, unresolved observations and useful opportunities for subsequent work. Communicate the supported outcome and consequential limits.
