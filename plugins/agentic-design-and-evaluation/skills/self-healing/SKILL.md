# Self-Healing

You heal the source of whatever made work cost more or end worse than it needed to. The source can be anything: an instruction, a skill, code, a tool, a hook, configuration, another repository, a mechanism that decides what judgment should decide, a default agents copy where a choice was needed, work that repeats and belongs in code, the way threads are run, or nothing at all.

## Observations

When something turns out differently than you expected, including your own progress, or the user corrects, questions, or re-prompts you, and it could matter beyond the moment, you SHALL leave an observation. It is free-form Markdown in your own words and gives, in this order: what happened, with exact text where text exists, and what you expected; how you read what guided you, meaning the instruction, request, plan, skill, or output you acted on, quoted with where it lives, what you took it to require, and what else in your context shaped that reading; and what you did. Explanations and remedies come after, marked as hypotheses, because what you write first shapes what you write after it.

Each problem has one observation file under `observations/` in the local context directory your environment uses for working records, outside the work's deliverables; a recurrence adds its evidence to that file. The current task's outcome stays first: you change the observed source during the task only when it blocks that outcome and the change meets the healing bar below.

## A healing pass

A pass runs when the user asks for one: a fresh session on Codex, Claude Code, or Gemini, loaded with this skill and pointed at what [scripts/gather.py](scripts/gather.py) gathered from recent sessions. [Running a pass](references/running-passes.md) covers starting one on each host and, only when the user asks for it, scheduling one. You SHALL NOT create recurring automation for passes unless the user asks for it.

**Evidence.** You SHALL form your reading from originals before earlier analyses: observations, session records, the user's own words, and the state work left behind. [scripts/gather.py](scripts/gather.py) reads recent sessions across hosts and writes an index and a faithful, timestamped transcript file per session, plus the git facts (branches, worktrees, stashes) and scheduled automations of the working directories they used; it extracts and quotes without classifying what a message means, so you SHALL read a session's own transcript before relying on an interpretation of it. For a host or OS surface gather.py has no reader for, find and read that host's own session files directly. The user's corrections and repeated "continue" are observations too, including of divergences the working agent never noticed. An agent's report of its own completion is a claim to check against the state it left.

**Findings.** A finding rests on recurrence across distinct sessions or on one incident severe enough alone. Recurrence shows that a symptom recurs, not what causes it, and repeats inside one runaway session are one piece of evidence. A finding states what was observed, with session ids and short quotes, before its explanation, and its explanation before any change. A finding that holds for some model families, sizes, or reasoning efforts and not others is recorded with what it held for among the differences between executors in the [foundational knowledge](../foundational-knowledge/references/foundational-knowledge.md) (17.5), where later passes recheck it against new models and add what they learn.

**Changes.** You SHALL change the maintained source of a cause, revising or deleting it in place: a correction added beside what it corrects leaves both in force, and removing what no longer earns its place is healing. A defect the evidence shows directly, such as instructions that contradict each other or the user's words, a stale fact, a failing or stale check, a broken command, or a mechanism the user's own words show deciding wrongly, you fix where it lives. A claim that wording or a mechanism changes agent behavior, where the evidence does not show it directly, you SHALL NOT act on until the problem reproduces N consecutive times under conditions you control, with N and the failure condition fixed before the first attempt; such a change is healed when the same conditions pass N consecutive times, a neighboring case it could affect still passes, and the reproducing scenario stays in the repository's evaluation suite as its regression check. A change to guidance heals only the agents that meet it, so it goes where the agents that met the problem meet it: what is always loaded, the entry of what they load for the task, or a step they already take. Before calling guidance ineffective, establish from exposure evidence whether the agent read it; guidance that was read and still not followed is healed by a mechanism at a step the agent takes, such as a required argument, a blocking check, or a review turn, not by restating it. A problem is put down to the model's own capability only when the guidance demonstrably reached it, such a mechanism was tried or is unavailable, and the same conditions pass on another model; until then its source is still being looked for. You SHALL fix a check before making the change it judges and SHALL NOT alter it to let the change pass. [Reproducing and verifying](references/reproducing-and-verifying.md) covers conditions, qualifying checks, the trial runtime, and evidence about how an agent read its instructions.

**End states.** When a pass ends, each problem it examined, and what the problem already produced (leftover branches, worktrees, automations, misleading records), SHALL stand in exactly one state: healed, with its evidence; deliberately unchanged, with the reason; or open, because resolving it needs a decision or authority the mandate does not give, named with who decides. A problem you can fix within the mandate is not open. Evidence that shows nothing wrong leaves the repository unchanged, adds no files, and raises no decisions. The observation store holds only what is open.

**Authority and delivery.** You SHALL act within the mandate. Rules about what agents may do without the user (merging, deploying, publishing, spending, credentials, approvals) and sources the user maintains verbatim change only through a proposal that carries its evidence. Each repository ends the pass with at most one unmerged `heal` branch of supported changes, landed only when the launch grants it, and nothing temporary of yours remains. Your report is your reply, or the file the launch names; it lists findings by their cost to the user, so every open decision can be made in one sitting.
