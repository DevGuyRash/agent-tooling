# Investigation and correction

Reconstruct the relevant assignment, original sources, consumer and operating conditions. Distinguish a changed requirement from drift, and a completed operation from a useful result reaching its recipient.

Seek observations that distinguish plausible explanations. Reproduce the consequential conditions and qualify the instrument against both inadequate outcomes and legitimate variations. Controlled reproduction supports deterministic defects; variable behavior calls for appropriate repeated observations and independent units. Retained incident counts can guide attention while their provenance and dependence remain visible.

An independent contributor can examine the original evidence, attempt reproduction and challenge the explanation. Resolve consequential differences through their grounds and the relevant decision rights. Preserve useful uncertainty when available evidence leaves several explanations open.

Assess where the useful change belongs. Information, access, coordination, instructions, code and interfaces can each affect the result. Examine a mechanism's assumptions and the choices it fixes. Preserve established requirements and give context-dependent choices to the executor who has the relevant information and authority.

For recurring effort, identify what stays stable, what varies legitimately and what the consumer needs. Compare the value of direct execution, existing capabilities and a maintained operation with their setup, context, failure and maintenance costs. A tool earns its place through its contribution to the work; removing unnecessary machinery can be the supported improvement.

Use maintained sources and retain consequential revisions. Check prepared changes against current inputs and preserve unrelated edits. Apply the access and effect boundaries the environment actually provides.

Verify the intervention's effect at the consumer and the neighboring behavior whose support changed. Further investigation should address a consequential unresolved question, changed evidence or an affected dependency. Retain supported no-change decisions and findings outside the current mandate for suitable subsequent work.
