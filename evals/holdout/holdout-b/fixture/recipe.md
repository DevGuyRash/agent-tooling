# Saffron Risotto — house recipe (serves 12)

Ingredients:

- Arborio rice: 1200 g
- Chicken stock: 3000 ml
- Butter: 150 g
- Parmesan cheese: 200 g
- Dry white wine: 300 ml
- Saffron threads: 1.5 g
- Yellow onion, diced: 300 g
- Olive oil: 60 ml

Method (unchanged for any batch size): sweat the onion in olive oil and half the
butter, toast the rice, deglaze with the wine, then add hot stock a ladle at a
time, stirring, with the saffron bloomed in the first ladle. Finish off the heat
with the remaining butter and the parmesan.
