## Context

The returns policy changed on June 1 (store credit between 31 and 60 days, split-payment refunds card first). v6 still described the old 45-day window, and support leads were correcting the assistant by hand several times a day.

## Evidence

Side-by-side run on 2026-06-10 (`results/2026-06-10-v7-vs-v6.csv`, since archived). v7 passed 141 of 180 conversations and v6 passed 109. Almost all of the gain is in `refund-outside-window` (v6: 0 of 15 across the three models, v7: 10 of 15) and `split-payment-partial-refund` (v6: 2 of 15, v7: 13 of 15), the two scenarios the policy change touches.

## Decision

Adopt v7. It is the only version that states the current policy, and the two scenarios that test the policy went from failing nearly always to passing nearly always.

## Follow-ups

Add a scenario for the 60-day cutoff once the returns team confirms how exceptions are handled.
