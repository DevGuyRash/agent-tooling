# What keeps going wrong

- When I come back and say "continue" or "keep going", they pick up whatever their own plan says is next instead of what I asked for. Sometimes what I asked for was already finished.
- Their notes, plans, and handoffs slowly turn into "the requirements", as if I had written them.
- They leave things everywhere: branches, extra checkouts, stashes, background processes, temp files.
- Some stop at a step only I can do and then keep retrying it or set something up to wait for it. Others just go ahead and do it without asking.
- They merge, publish, or send things I never said to.
- They say they're done when it doesn't actually work yet, or they skip the checks after a change.
- They set up recurring jobs or heartbeats to keep themselves going for days.
- When what I asked for won't get me what I'm after, they do it anyway and don't mention it.
