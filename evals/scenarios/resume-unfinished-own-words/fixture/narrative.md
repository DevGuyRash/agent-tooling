# Program Narrative — Riverside Community Garden

Riverside Community Garden turns a half-acre of vacant lot on Delancey Street into eighteen raised-bed plots, a shared herb border, and a rainwater catchment system serving forty member households. Since opening in 2024, the garden has hosted a weekly produce swap, an after-school planting club with Riverside Elementary, and two seasonal workdays that drew over sixty volunteers.

This grant would fund the garden's fall-to-summer operating cycle: replacing exhausted soil in the raised beds, buying seeds and seedlings for a three-season rotation, repairing the drip-irrigation lines damaged by last winter's freeze, and covering a part-time volunteer coordinator stipend so the produce swap and planting club can keep their current schedule.

The garden's steering committee — five resident volunteers elected each spring — will oversee spending and report outcomes to members at the June annual meeting, which will double as this grant's final report to the funder.
