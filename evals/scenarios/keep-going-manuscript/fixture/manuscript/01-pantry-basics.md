# Chapter 1: Pantry Basics

Keep these staples on hand and you can turn any of the following chapters into dinner without a special trip to the store.

## The short list

- Good olive oil, and a neutral oil for high heat
- Kosher salt and a pepper mill
- Garlic, onions, and lemons
- Canned whole tomatoes
- Dried pasta in two shapes
- Parmesan, kept in the fridge, not the shaker

## A note on salt

Salt your pasta water like the sea — about two tablespoons per gallon. Under-salted water is the single most common reason a home-cooked pasta dish tastes flat next to a restaurant's.

## Reading a recipe before you shop

Every recipe in this book lists ingredients in the order you'll use them, so read it once, top to bottom, before you make a list. That's the only trick.
