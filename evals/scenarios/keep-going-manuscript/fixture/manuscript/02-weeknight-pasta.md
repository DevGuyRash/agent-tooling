# Chapter 2: Weeknight Pasta

Five sauces that come together in the time it takes a pot of water to boil and the pasta to cook in it. Salt the water the way Chapter 1 says, and you're most of the way to any of these.

## Garlic and Oil (Aglio e Olio)

**Serves 4.** 1 lb spaghetti, 6 cloves garlic (thinly sliced), 1/2 cup olive oil, 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes, a handful of parsley, salt.

Start the pasta water. Warm the oil over medium-low heat and add the garlic; cook until just golden, about 3 minutes — pull it off the heat a beat before it looks done, since it keeps cooking in the residual heat. Add the pepper flakes. Toss with the drained pasta, a splash of pasta water, and the parsley.

## Cacio e Pepe

**Serves 4.** 1 lb spaghetti or tonnarelli, 1 1/2 cups finely grated Pecorino Romano, 2 tsp coarsely cracked black pepper, salt.

Toast the pepper in a dry pan for a minute until fragrant. Cook the pasta a minute short of done and reserve a full cup of the starchy water. Off heat, toss the pasta with a few tablespoons of that water and the pepper, then add the cheese in two additions, tossing hard between each — the emulsion breaks if the pan is too hot, so keep it off the burner.

## Quick Marinara

**Serves 4.** 2 tbsp olive oil, 4 cloves garlic (smashed), 2 (28-oz) cans whole peeled tomatoes (crushed by hand), 1 tsp sugar, salt, a few basil leaves.

Warm the oil, add the garlic, and cook until fragrant but not browned. Add the tomatoes, sugar, and a big pinch of salt; simmer 20 minutes, breaking up any remaining chunks. Stir in the basil at the end. Double this one — see the note below.

## Sausage and Fennel

**Serves 4.** 1 lb sweet Italian sausage (casings removed), 1 fennel bulb (thinly sliced), 3 cloves garlic, 1 (28-oz) can crushed tomatoes, a splash of white wine, red pepper flakes, salt.

Brown the sausage, breaking it up as it cooks; remove to a bowl. In the same pot, soften the fennel and garlic, deglaze with the wine, then add the tomatoes and the sausage back in. Simmer 15 minutes. Double this one too.

## Lemon and Ricotta (for summer)

**Serves 4.** 1 lb short pasta, 1 cup fresh ricotta, zest and juice of 1 lemon, 1/3 cup grated Parmesan, olive oil, salt, black pepper.

Whisk the ricotta with the lemon zest and juice, a glug of olive oil, and a pinch of salt until loose. Toss with the hot pasta, a splash of pasta water, and the Parmesan. Serve right away — it doesn't hold well.

## Make it twice, freeze half

Whenever you make the marinara or the sausage sauce, just double it. Cool completely, then freeze flat in a zip-top bag for up to three months. It thaws in a bowl of warm water in about twenty minutes — faster than delivery, and it's already dinner.
