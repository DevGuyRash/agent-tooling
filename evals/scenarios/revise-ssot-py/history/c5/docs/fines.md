# Overdue fines

Approved by the library board on 8 April 2026, in effect from 1 May 2026. This is the policy the circulation desk, the overnight notices, and the kiosk all charge.

## How much

| Category | What it covers | Per day late | Most one item can owe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `adult` | adult books, audiobooks, magazines | 0.30 | 7.00 |
| `children` | children's and teen books | 0.15 | 3.00 |
| `media` | DVDs, Blu-rays, video games | 1.25 | 12.50 |

The category is the `category` column of the loans export (docs/export-format.md).

## Days late and the grace period

An item is as many days late as there are calendar days from its due date to the day it came back, or, for an item still out, to the day the fine is worked out for. An item back on or before its due date is not late.

An item 2 days late or less owes nothing: that is the grace period, and the overnight notices leave such items out. From the third day late every day counts, the first two included: a children's book back 5 days late owes 5 × 0.15 = 0.75.

An item never owes more than the most for its category: a DVD back 12 days late owes 12.50, not 15.00.

## Blocked cards

A card owing 10.00 or more in total cannot borrow until it owes less. The kiosk says so.

## Changes

- 1 May 2026: the most a children's item can owe is 3.00 (was 2.50).
- 1 February 2026: the grace period is 2 days (was 3). The most a media item can owe is 12.50 (was 10.00).
