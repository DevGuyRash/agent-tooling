Route one Northgate Logistics IT ticket (requester, department, text). Output a single JSON object, no other text:

{queue: identity|endpoint|network|facilities-av|security|procurement, priority: P1|P2|P3, needs_human: bool, summary: string <= 20 words, no requester name}

Queues:
- identity: passwords, MFA, lockouts, new accounts, shared mailbox or folder access, leavers.
- endpoint: laptops, desktops, phones, printers, peripherals, software on a device.
- network: VPN, Wi-Fi, office network, remote access, slow connections.
- facilities-av: meeting-room screens and audio, badge readers, door access.
- security: phishing, suspected compromise, lost or stolen devices, restores that may involve deleted evidence.
- procurement: buying hardware or licenses, renewals needing a purchase order.
Several fit: pick the one that must act first (new hire laptop + account: identity).

Priority: P1 cannot work with no workaround, or possible security incident (phishing is always P1). P2 core tool broken or deadline within 2 working days at risk. P3 otherwise; future dates are P3 unless within 2 working days.

needs_human: true for money (purchases, renewals over $500), access for someone else, legal or HR matters, or low confidence.

Empty or unreadable ticket: identity, P3, needs_human true, summary says it could not be read.
