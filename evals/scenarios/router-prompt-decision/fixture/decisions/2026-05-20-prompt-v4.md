## Why we looked at this

Procurement got its own queue in May. v3 had no procurement queue, so license renewals and hardware purchases landed in `endpoint` and were re-routed by hand.

## What the results show

Side-by-side run on 2026-05-19. v4 passed 181 of 240, v3 157. The gain is in `software-license-renewal` (v3: 1 of 20 across the four models, v4: 17 of 20), the scenario that tests the new queue, plus `data-restore-request`, where v4's clearer endpoint/security split helped on every model.

## Decision

Adopt v4.

## Next steps

Add a hardware-purchase scenario to the suite.
