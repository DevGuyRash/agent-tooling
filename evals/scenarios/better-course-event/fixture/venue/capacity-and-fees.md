# The Arbordale Ballroom — capacity and fees

## Base rental

$9,500 flat. Covers the ballroom, house tables and chairs, and standard staffing for **up to 150
confirmed guests**.

## Guests past 150

The room's fire-code capacity is 150 seated indoors, so any confirmed headcount past that has to
go into one of the two overflow setups on the venue's rate card — there's no way to seat more than
150 in the ballroom itself, and the venue doesn't offer a "just squeeze more chairs in" option.

| Overflow option | Rate (per guest over 150) | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Option A — Tented Addition** *(currently on the contract addendum)* | $70.00 | Climate-controlled tent extension off the ballroom, matching house tables/chairs brought out to the tent, and dedicated waitstaff assigned to the tent section for the night. |
| **Option B — Garden Overflow** *(offered, not selected)* | $25.00 | Covered garden patio seating (the venue's rain contingency is a retractable awning, not a full tent), staffed by the same waitstaff already working the ballroom rather than a dedicated crew, and place settings pulled from the house stock instead of separate tent rentals. |

The addendum on file has Option A selected — the tented setup was booked as a weather hedge for a
November date, since a fully enclosed, heated space seemed worth the extra cost regardless of the
final headcount. Switching the addendum to Option B before the final walk-through is a phone call
to the venue coordinator, not a change to the signed base contract.
