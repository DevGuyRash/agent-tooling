Riverside Hall

20 River Walk, Riverside. Doors open at 08:30 on both days.

Getting here

Tram line 4 stops at Riverside Square, a five-minute level walk from the hall. Bicycle parking is beside the north entrance.

Step-free access

A separate entrance

Allow five extra minutes.

Use the EAST entrance on Willow Lane, beside the blue lift sign. The River Walk steps are not the accessible route.

For assistance at the entrance, call the demo desk at 555-0107.

During the event

All session rooms have level access. A quiet room is available beside reception. Bring a reusable bottle; refill points are marked on each floor.
