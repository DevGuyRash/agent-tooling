# City Exchange product brief

City Exchange is a two-day event on 14 and 15 October at Riverside Hall. Visitors use the website to choose a day, understand its sessions, inspect rooms and speakers, and find travel and accessibility information. Day one asks how people shape shared places; day two asks how ideas become everyday practice.

The program contains morning and afternoon sessions plus an end-of-day reflection. Every displayed Details link belongs to a particular session. Both days and the practical venue information should remain usable on a desktop and a phone. Visitors need the full step-free entrance directions to arrive at the correct entrance.

The site is a synthetic fixture: no real registration, payment, or contact is required. Presentation and navigation are the review focus; concise representative evidence should let the event team understand and reproduce consequential findings.
