Why the results differ

deadline_first.py considers jobs in order A, C, B, D. After completing A at time 3, C would finish at time 5, past its deadline of 4, so it skips C without advancing time. B and D then finish exactly at their deadlines.

value_first.py considers jobs in order B, C, A, D. B finishes at time 2 and C at time 4, both on time. It skips A because A would finish at time 7, past its deadline of 3, then completes D at time 5.

Both earn B’s and D’s values; value-first additionally earns C’s 35 instead of A’s 20, producing the 15-unit advantage.
