# ADR 0027: Keep evidence and improvement together, with separate visual authoring

- Status: Accepted; [ADR 0028](0028-behavior-evidence-and-rebuilt-entries.md) supersedes its Self-Healing design and its rule that kept the foundation and authoring guidance verbatim.
- Scope: Agentic Design & Evaluation and Visualization package ownership.

## Context

Observation capture, investigation and supported correction share evidence and authority boundaries. A separate mending workflow imposed closure and duplicated storage mechanics. The general visual authoring skill serves broader outputs than diagrams, while Mermaid source authoring does not require Split Testing's HTML library.

## Decision

Agentic Design & Evaluation delivers five independently callable skills: Foundational Knowledge, Prompt and Context Design, Skill Auditor, Split Testing and Self-Healing. Split Testing owns comparative methodology.

Visualization independently delivers Visual Authoring and Mermaid with focused references and no bundled renderer or report framework. Split Testing retains the existing visual library and its public delivery interfaces. Optional visual contributions do not become mandatory stages or a fixed ontology.

## Consequences

Installed consumers receive complete declared resources. Migration verifies the qualified replacement and a fresh host session before removing old selected installation declarations; historical records and unrelated installations are preserved. Recurring maintenance requires an available scheduling capability and an explicit delegated scope. Mechanisms serve defined interfaces while the executor owns contextual interpretation and decisions.

Physical record order and append-only interpretation history support recoverable evidence; they do not establish private reasoning order. Structural checks and local examples do not establish general behavioral superiority. Source changes remain candidates until their intended outcomes are qualified.
