# ADR 0023: Narrow assurance when coverage does not improve

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-17

## Decision

When repeated held-out evidence shows that an instruction system improves useful work but does not reliably observe a consequential boundary, narrow its external assurance claim to the boundary the evidence supports. Do not convert a broader aspiration into a maker promise, and do not add another coverage clause unless an intervention shows that the clause changes the missing behavior without a material regression.

The internal system may continue seeking broad material evidence. Claim narrowing changes what a consumer is entitled to infer; it does not require weakening useful discovery, verification, or honest residual-risk behavior.

## Why

The revised Skill Auditor materially improved authority judgment and no-clobber discovery, and won two of three fact-checked comparisons against its prior draft. Across all compared audits, however, it missed a reachable destructive input/output identity collapse. A direct composition instruction found the defect in only one of three fresh runs while adding about 18 percent mean cost. A separate fresh-challenge clause found it in none of three runs and lost a previously stable finding once.

Those results support an evidence-driven material audit, not comprehensive release certification. Keeping the broader description would turn an aspiration into unsupported product assurance; adding more words had already failed the outcome gate twice.

## Consequences

Skill Auditor 3.2 advertises material evidence, evidence-driven quality review, and release-risk investigation. Its reports preserve inspected boundaries and residual risk rather than certifying the absence of undiscovered defects. A future broader claim requires a distinct observation mechanism and fresh evidence on both the known missed boundary and dissimilar held-out work.

Reopen this decision when a behaviorally tested mechanism reliably expands discovery without losing existing material coverage or importing ambient authority.
