# ADR 0009: Establish consumer semantics before contract findings

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-15

## Decision

Before treating a declaration, path, field, permission, command, or artifact as a binding executable contract, establish the consumer that gives it operational meaning and the authority that defines that meaning. Adoption may be evidenced by a governing schema, maker-set target prose, or observed consumer behavior. Familiar spelling, conventional placement, structural resemblance, or successful direct invocation does not supply missing consumer semantics.

Once adoption is established, judge the exact relationship it declares and trace consequential capability claims through the real owner or adapter to an observable result. Keep an unresolved relationship conditional and state the evidence that would settle it. Do not discard source-level inconsistencies merely because the live consumer is unavailable, but do not promote them into host or installation failures without the missing contract.

Unknown external enforcement and an internal target contradiction are separate conclusions. When a target itself declares an authority boundary, supported environment, or capability and its reachable implementation crosses it, report that target-owned mismatch at the strength of the declaration. Only the external host's enforcement consequence remains conditional.

A target-authored allowed set or limiting declaration supplies its plain stated scope even when the consumer is unavailable. Consumer evidence is still required for sandboxing, rejection, transformation, or semantics the target did not express; it is not required to compare that allowed set with the target's own default reachable path.

## Why

In the corrected blind Braid audit comparison, every redevelopment run found the two demonstrated collector defects and held release, yet unguided audits were still preferred in four of six reviews. Redeveloped audits repeatedly treated an undocumented custom `files` list as an exhaustive package inventory or treated failure under direct command execution as proof of host failure. The target did not provide those loader or invocation semantics. One run also missed that named capability files and a shared collector did not establish that a published capability was actually selected and fulfilled.

These were not missing inventory items. They arose from collapsing four different facts: an artifact exists, a contract adopts it, a consumer interprets it, and the resulting path achieves a promised outcome. Adding more field names or host cases would encode the holdout and leave the foundational mistake intact. Requiring the authority link and observable handoff addresses the shared cause while preserving unanticipated package designs.

A follow-up candidate reversed the earlier Braid preference pattern in four blind reviews and tied a fifth, while one reviewer still preferred the prior auditor. The non-wins exposed the other side of the same distinction: some audits correctly avoided declaring an unknown host layer broken but then under-reported that the published capability was still unproven for release. That round also exposed a permission-mode transformation in the review view, so it is mechanism evidence rather than final confirmation. The instruction now separates established failure from failure to establish readiness.

A later exact-candidate run preserved file modes and used a position-identifiable rereview. Every revised audit again found both unconditional executable defects. Two of three revised runs correctly kept undocumented host composition conditional; one still widened a component-output mismatch into a High end-to-end capability finding. All unguided controls made at least one comparable widening error, yet most semantic reviewers preferred them for broader coverage. This established both sides of the boundary: evidence must not widen across a handoff, and an unknown handoff must not disappear into a generic uncertainty when independently promised outcomes need different evidence or repairs.

A subsequent local-plugin audit exposed the opposite overcorrection. Three fresh auditors observed that configuration could select an arbitrary executable and non-loopback vault URL even though the target manifest declared a single process path and loopback-only network access. Each demoted the mismatch because the consuming host's permission schema was unavailable. The host-enforcement claim was indeed unverified, but the target owned both the declaration and the reachable implementation; external uncertainty did not erase that internal release contradiction. The next intervention separated those two conclusions instead of restoring a blanket assumption about familiar manifest fields.

The exposing rerun then found a second, independent authority error. Broader auditors discovered more real failure and retry behavior, but one promoted medium and low/medium operational risks into P1 blockers without a target-owned atomicity or idempotency promise. Raw blind review preferred the older concise audits four times out of six. Condition-blind truth checks later found that all four older-audit preferences also discounted validated High or Medium omissions; the broader audits were more materially complete, but their extra observations still needed lower severity. The supported repair is therefore not “find less” or “treat every boundary as a blocker.” Discovery scope and disposition are separate: preserve the observed boundary, then classify it only at the strength of adopted authority and demonstrated consequence.

The first calibration intervention overcorrected. Three fresh audits stopped promoting retry and failure observations into unadopted guarantees, but all three then omitted the demonstrated duplicate-publication and stale-state consequences from their material findings. They also continued to make the target's process and network declaration/runtime conflicts conditional on unknown host enforcement. The follow-up correction separates both pairs of claims: absence of a named reliability property does not erase a reachable harmful state, and unknown external enforcement does not erase an ordinary-language conflict among the target's own distributed surfaces. Each conclusion remains bounded to the consequence its evidence actually establishes.

The Dispatch plugin exposed a related version-of-authority boundary. Its installed repository validator accepted the Codex manifest, but the current official submission contract rejected two host-owned values. Neither result invalidated the other: the first established compatibility with one implemented checker, while the second controlled the current publication claim. The audit must identify the intended consumer and horizon, reconcile the host-facing object against that authority, and avoid turning a partial or older pass into global readiness.

The later matched Dispatch round exposed an incorporation boundary. Three blind reviewers dismissed an absent-license-text finding because the target did not separately state that a notice file was required. The manifests' `MIT` declaration was itself the relevant adoption: the referenced MIT terms condition permission on carrying the copyright and permission notices with delivered copies or substantial portions. Independent fact-checking restored the scoped distribution-readiness concern while rejecting claims about past recipients, ownership, or delivery facts the snapshot could not establish. A declaration may incorporate external authority; it neither makes that authority optional hygiene nor imports unrelated ecosystem preference.

The next three-way comparison exposed the complementary payload boundary. Some audits correctly followed the `MIT` declaration to its incorporated terms but then treated the inspected snapshot as the complete distribution payload without establishing that fact. Other reviewers discarded the incorporated obligation entirely. Both moves were unsound. Adoption determines which terms govern; the actual or intended delivery boundary determines whether the observed payload satisfies them. If payload completeness is unresolved, the consequence remains conditional rather than becoming either a present blocker or a non-issue.

Fresh follow-up audits then recovered the payload condition but repeatedly phrased the repair as a required plugin-root `LICENSE` file. Independent source checks preserved the missing-terms finding and rejected the layout requirement: the incorporated MIT terms require the notice and permission text to accompany distributed copies, not one universal filename or source-tree location. A familiar file is a good implementation when the actual packaging boundary carries it; the binding repair is the delivered outcome.

## Consequences

Audits and implementation reviews distinguish source observations, adopted contracts, consumer behavior, and outcome evidence whenever collapsing them could change severity, disposition, or remediation. Direct probes are scoped to the mode they actually exercised. Capability claims require reachable outcome evidence at the claimed boundary, while unavailable host composition remains a visible condition rather than a guessed fact.

A component result supports an end-to-end conclusion only when the adopted composition makes that component necessary to the published outcome under the relevant invocation and collection semantics. Different promises remain separately decision-complete when different evidence could change their status or repair; this is an outcome requirement, not a catalog of package surfaces.

An unresolved consumer may prevent a defect claim without permitting a positive readiness claim. Reports keep those conclusions separate so uncertainty cannot become either a fabricated blocker or false assurance.

The fact that a transition deserves verification does not create a missing guarantee. Audits retain valid boundary observations while independently calibrating whether each is a defect, a release risk, a contract decision, or an unverified consequence. Broader discovery does not raise severity.

This decision can reduce apparent finding count while improving correctness. Reopen it when a host schema, package format, or runtime supplies a stronger portable rule that makes a formerly conditional relationship unconditional, or when behavioral evidence shows the distinction itself causes repeated material misses.
