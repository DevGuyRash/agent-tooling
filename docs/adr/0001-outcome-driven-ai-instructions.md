# ADR 0001: Bind AI instruction outcomes, not unneeded pathways

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-08-15

## Decision

AI-facing instructions in this repository define the mission, non-inferable reality and authority, material boundaries, persistence, verification, precedence, and handoff needed for success. They leave reasoning and implementation routes open unless a maker-set interface, external contract, named hazard, or observed model failure makes a route load-bearing.

The governing architecture in [`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md) is the normative expression of this decision. Skills, tests, and audits must not convert its semantic functions into mandatory headings, reasoning rituals, report sections, exhaustive taxonomies, or visible proof that deliberation occurred.

## Why

The executor's intelligence is useful only while compliant alternatives remain reachable. Procedure that exists mainly to make compliance visible consumes context, displaces task grounding, and can make a plausible artifact easier to produce than the correct outcome. Exact procedures are still valuable when their order or mechanism carries an actual requirement or hazard.

The frozen Skill Auditor draft demonstrated the failure locally: it rewarded named methods and document shape while missing executable behavior. Comparative studies also report grounding and verification displacement and expensive procedural detours ([The Regression Tax](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22520v1); [Agent Skills Can Be Harmful](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11888v1)). Those findings justify testing for the effect; they do not make procedure inherently defective.

A later Relay intervention required complementary discovery by separate inspectors. All three fresh auditors then claimed independent coverage without having created the stated independent context. Replacing that topology requirement with the observable coverage outcome eliminated the invented provenance in all three follow-up audits while preserving the verified hard findings. The failure was caused by turning an evidentiary property into a visible ritual; adding stricter wording about the same ritual would not have corrected it.

## Consequences

Review instruction systems by the behavior and authority their controls produce. A plan, rubric, checklist, example, script, or exact sequence earns its place through a real interface, hazard, continuity, or verification need—not through convention alone. Remove a control when its absence would not weaken mission, authority, safety, continuity, correctness, or handoff.

Reopen a specific open route when repeated target evidence shows a material failure that a bounded instruction reliably corrects. Keep that compensation separate and record the evidence and removal condition instead of making it universal.
